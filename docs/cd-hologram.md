# CD Hologram

Requires SDEC 2.1.0 or newer. This is an authored view-dependent optical effect, not a live reflection of the game world. Preview lighting is approximate.

Choose **CD Hologram** as its own effect. Supply artwork and a depth image. Full CD art has a clamp hub; Corner CD art places the virtual disc centre outside the deck. Artwork colours can be separate from the backing rainbow or shared.

The backing choices preserve Mirror silver and Dyed silver and add the current CD mirror. CD mirror offers Original, Mirror contrast, Stronger diffraction, and Combined responses. Original preserves the approved baseline. Stronger backing rainbow is optional. Clear-layer white sheen can be disabled or adjusted separately.

Artwork depth adjusts only the depth signal. Faint artwork visibility controls the quiet image outside the main response. Original image pixels are not regenerated.

Clear rim, recording-area boundary and hairline scratches are optional and default off. Strength can reach 400% of the subtle test. Scratch amount selects zero to four fixed hairlines. These are surface details, not additional image layers or raised engraving.

Other effects have optional white sheen. Smooth Depth Map, Parallax without Enable lenticular, and Ghost Rare use smooth transport. Enable lenticular retains intentional lens banding. Ghost Rare preserves the existing depth direction.
